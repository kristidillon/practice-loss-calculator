Sources and assumptions

Where the numbers come from.

Wages — BLS OEWS, May 2024

Dental hygienists (SOC 29-1292) and dental assistants (SOC 31-9091) are official published mean hourly wages, cross-industry, for all 50 states and the District of Columbia. Receptionists (SOC 43-4171) are mean hourly wages for Offices of Dentists (NAICS 621200). State receptionist figures are May 2024 OEWS research estimates: BLS publishes them for research, they do not get every quality check of the standard release, and model error may be higher. The national receptionist figure is the official national industry mean, $21.39. Team/hr is one of each role. Wages only; benefits and payroll taxes are not included. Minnesota, Montana, and Vermont have no published dental-office receptionist wage, so Team/hr is n/a there and the estimate uses the U.S. receptionist mean, labeled as such. BLS OEWS tables. May 2024 research estimates.

Production and hours — ADA Health Policy Institute, 2025

2026 Survey of Dental Practice, results for 2025. Table 9: solo general practitioners, average gross billings per practice hour $621.15 (median $529.15, 246 responses). That is practice billings per practice hour, not a dentist-only wage, and it is national only. Table 19: owner general practitioners averaged 1,726 hours in the office in 2025. ADA dental practice research.

Overhead — derived, not an ADA-published percent

Table 13: average practice expenses per owner general practitioner, excluding shareholder salaries, $556,450 (367 responses). Table 5: average gross billings per owner general practitioner, $965,660 (423 responses). 556,450 / 965,660 = 57.62% rounded to the cent. Gross billings are not collections, the two averages are from different respondents, and owner pay is excluded. Per practice hour applies that percent to the production-per-hour field. At the ADA hourly average, that is about $358. That share is shown next to lost production and is not added to the annual total, because gross billings per hour already include overhead.

Studies you can apply, not the starting case volume

McCracken et al., Journal of Prosthodontics 28(2):122–130, published online November 22, 2018. Of 3,731 single-unit crowns that reached try-in, 141 (3.8%) were rejected. 205 dentists in the National Dental Practice-Based Research Network. doi:10.1111/jopr.12995. The remake field starts at 8% because that is the one-case calculator’s starting value, not this study.

McCracken et al., Journal of the American Dental Association 2017;148(11):788–796.e4. Dentists in that questionnaire reported a mean of 18 crowns a month. Most (59%) reported a remake rate under 2%, and 17% reported a rate over 4%. That is self-report, separate from the 3.8% try-in study. PMC5793929. Units per month start at 25, the one-case calculator’s starting value.

ZIP codes

GeoNames postal codes, CC BY 4.0, U.S. file. geonames.org. A ZIP maps to a state, then to that state’s OEWS wages. It is not a ZIP-level wage. Military mail entries in that file have no state, so the page uses U.S. means. Puerto Rico is not in this wage file. Two Hawaii ZIP codes that also appear as military mail, 96860 and 96863, map to Hawaii.

Comparison formula

The lower comparison prices the gap in remake rate, adjustment minutes, and chasing hours. It does not include overhead, lab fees, or turnaround. A 5–10% industry-average remake line is not used here; this page did not find a public source for that range.

Marked “assumption — enter your own”

  • Adjustment minutes, prep minutes, seat minutes, and chair hours per remake.
  • Assistant, hygienist, and receptionist hours per chair hour start at 1, which is Team/hr. Extra hygienist hours per remake and per seat start at 0.
  • Lab fee per remake and temp cost (both start at $0).
  • Extra visits (starts at 1), the day threshold (7), and minutes per extra visit (20).
  • Carrying rate (starts at 0, so the queue of billings is shown and not added).
  • Hours per day (8). Turnaround days (14). The scenario’s 2%, 5 minutes, and 7 days.

The annual total is lost production plus staff wages for that unproductive time. Overhead is not added again. Lab fees, temps, and carrying cost are added only when those fields are set. Estimates update as you type. They are not a quote.
